Dead Cells Game Review

Beheaded

Dead Cells is a 2018 roguelike-Metroidvania game developed by Motion Twin. The player takes the role of an amorphous creature called the Prisoner. As the Prisoner, the player must fight their way out of a diseased island in order to slay the island's King. The player gains weapons, treasure and other tools through exploration of the procedurally generated levels.

My game review will cover the gameplay in Dead Cells, Items/weapons found in the game, design of the levels and the final review.
